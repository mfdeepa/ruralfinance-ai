GOVERNMENT BANKING AND FINANCIAL SCHEMES — RuralFinance AI Knowledge Base

PM JAN DHAN YOJANA (PMJDY): Zero-balance savings account for every Indian. Free RuPay debit card. Rs 1 lakh accidental insurance. Rs 30,000 life insurance. Overdraft up to Rs 10,000. Available at all banks and Business Correspondents. Opened at nearest bank or BC agent with Aadhaar.

KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC): For farmers. Flexible revolving credit for agriculture needs (seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, equipment). Low interest (7% with government subsidy). Repay after harvest. Covers post-harvest expenses too. Apply at any bank with land documents.

PM MUDRA YOJANA (PMMY): Loans for small businesses and self-employment. Three categories: Shishu (up to Rs 50,000), Kishore (Rs 50,000 to Rs 5 lakh), Tarun (Rs 5 lakh to Rs 10 lakh). No collateral for smaller amounts. For: street vendors, small shops, artisans, small manufacturers. Apply at any bank or NBFC.

PM SVANidhi: Working capital loans for street vendors. Rs 10,000 first loan. Rs 20,000 after timely repayment. Rs 50,000 further. No collateral. 7% interest subsidy. Digital transactions give cashback. Apply through city municipal office or ULB app.

STAND UP INDIA: Loans from Rs 10 lakh to Rs 1 crore for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs. To set up new enterprise. At least one SC/ST and one woman borrower per bank branch. Collateral-free under CGFMU guarantee.

STARTUP INDIA: Government support for startups. Tax benefits, easier regulations, funding access. Register at startupindia.gov.in.

PM FASAL BIMA YOJANA (PMFBY): Crop insurance for farmers. Very low premium (1.5% for kharif, 2% for rabi, 5% for commercial crops). Government pays remaining premium. Covers losses from drought, flood, cyclone, unseasonal rain, pests. Compulsory for KCC loan holders. Apply through bank or Common Service Centre (CSC).

PRADHAN MANTRI JEEVAN JYOTI BIMA YOJANA (PMJJBY): Life insurance. Premium Rs 436/year (auto-deducted from bank account). Coverage Rs 2 lakh on death from any cause. Age 18-50. Available through any bank account. Renew every year.

PRADHAN MANTRI SURAKSHA BIMA YOJANA (PMSBY): Accident insurance. Premium only Rs 20/year. Rs 2 lakh on accidental death or total permanent disability. Rs 1 lakh on partial permanent disability. Age 18-70. Available through bank account. Renew every June.

ATAL PENSION YOJANA (APY): Pension scheme for unorganized sector workers. Guaranteed monthly pension of Rs 1,000 to Rs 5,000 after age 60. Government contributes 50% of contribution (up to Rs 1,000/year) for new subscribers till 31.3.2015. Age 18-40. Apply through bank. Premium based on joining age and pension amount chosen.

PM VAYA VANDANA YOJANA (PMVVY): For senior citizens (60+). Invest lump sum with LIC. Get guaranteed monthly pension of 7.4% per year for 10 years. Maximum investment Rs 15 lakh. Safe government-backed pension.

NPS (National Pension System): Pension savings scheme for everyone. Two accounts: Tier I (pension, tax benefit, locked till 60) and Tier II (flexible savings, no tax benefit). On retirement, 60% withdrawn tax-free, 40% used to buy annuity (monthly pension). Very low charges. Best for long-term retirement planning.

EPCF (Employees' Provident Fund): For salaried employees. Both employee and employer contribute 12% of basic salary each. Government adds 8.33% for pension (EPS). Tax-free returns around 8.1% per year. Cannot withdraw freely before retirement. Very safe.

PPF (Public Provident Fund): For self-employed and salaried. 15-year lock-in. Rs 500 minimum to Rs 1.5 lakh maximum per year. Interest 7.1% tax-free. Section 80C benefit. Partial withdrawal allowed after 7 years.

SUKANYA SAMRIDDHI YOJANA (SSY): For girl child. Open before girl turns 10. 21-year duration or marriage after 18. Current interest 8.2%. Maximum Rs 1.5 lakh per year. Full tax exemption (EEE). Excellent for daughters' higher education and marriage expenses.

FINANCIAL LITERACY PROGRAMME (FLP): RBI and NABARD run free financial literacy camps in rural areas. Free booklets in local languages on banking, savings, loans, insurance. Contact local bank branch or NABARD district office.

BUSINESS CORRESPONDENT (BC): Agent appointed by bank to provide banking services in areas without a bank branch. BC uses a micro-ATM device. Can open accounts, accept deposits, allow withdrawals, give loans. Your rural Bank Mitra or BC agent is a BC.

COMMON SERVICE CENTRES (CSC): Village-level entrepreneur providing government services including banking, insurance, pensions, certificates. Found in most gram panchayats. Digital Seva Kendra.

DIRECT BENEFIT TRANSFER (DBT): Government subsidies and welfare payments sent directly to your bank account. No middlemen. Fertilizer subsidy, scholarship, LPG subsidy, MGNREGA wages all come through DBT. Requires Aadhaar linked to bank account.

MGNREGA: Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act. Guarantees 100 days of wage employment to rural households per year. Wages paid directly to bank account. Implemented by panchayats.

PM KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI: Rs 6,000 per year to small and marginal farmers in three equal installments. Direct to bank account. Register through local agriculture department or CSC.

SOIL HEALTH CARD: Free soil testing for farmers. Tells you what nutrients your soil needs. Saves money on fertilizers by applying only what is needed.

NATIONAL RURAL LIVELIHOOD MISSION (NRLM - Aajeevika): Promotes formation of Self Help Groups (SHGs) of rural women. Provides training, credit, and market access. SHGs get preferential loans at 7% interest. Women's empowerment through financial inclusion.

PMAY (Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana): Affordable housing scheme. Subsidy on home loan interest (up to Rs 2.67 lakh for economically weaker sections). For first-time home buyers. Apply through banks or urban local bodies.

JAL JEEVAN MISSION: Tap water connections to every rural household. Not directly financial but reduces water-fetching burden, especially for women, enabling more productive time.

E-SHRAM CARD: Registration for unorganized sector workers. Get unique worker ID. Accident insurance Rs 2 lakh. Future access to government schemes. Register at eshram.gov.in or nearest CSC.

AADHAR-ENABLED PAYMENT SYSTEM (AePS): Withdraw cash, check balance using only Aadhaar number + fingerprint. No card needed. Works at any micro-ATM with BC agent. Safe for elderly and illiterate people.

PMEGP (PM Employment Generation Programme): Subsidy for manufacturing/service enterprises. Subsidy 15-35% of project cost depending on location and category. Maximum project cost Rs 25 lakh (manufacturing) and Rs 10 lakh (service).

CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises): Government guarantee for loans up to Rs 2 crore to small businesses. Banks give collateral-free loans because government guarantees repayment. Mudra loans and Stand Up India loans covered.
