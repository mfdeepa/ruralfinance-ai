BANKING BASICS — RuralFinance AI Knowledge Base

WHAT IS A BANK?
A bank is an institution that keeps your money safe and helps you grow it. Banks are regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). Your money deposited in a bank is insured up to ₹5 lakh by DICGC (Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation). This means even if the bank fails, you get your money back up to ₹5 lakh.

TYPES OF BANK ACCOUNTS

1. SAVINGS ACCOUNT
Purpose: Everyday savings and regular transactions.
Interest Rate: 2.5% to 4% per year.
Minimum Balance: ₹500 to ₹5,000 (Jan Dhan accounts have zero minimum balance).
Key Features: Debit card, mobile banking, limited free ATM withdrawals (usually 5/month at own bank ATM).
Best For: Salaried employees, students, homemakers, farmers.

2. CURRENT ACCOUNT
Purpose: High-volume business transactions.
Interest Rate: 0% (no interest paid).
Minimum Balance: ₹5,000 to ₹25,000.
Key Features: Unlimited transactions, overdraft facility available.
Best For: Business owners, traders, self-employed professionals.

3. FIXED DEPOSIT (FD)
Purpose: Save a lump sum for a fixed period and earn higher interest.
Interest Rate: 5.5% to 7.5% per year (senior citizens get 0.5% extra).
Minimum Amount: Usually ₹1,000.
Tenure: 7 days to 10 years.
Key Features: Guaranteed returns, loan against FD available, penalty for premature withdrawal.
Best For: Risk-averse savers, people with lump sums to invest.

4. RECURRING DEPOSIT (RD)
Purpose: Build savings discipline by depositing a fixed amount monthly.
Interest Rate: 5% to 7% per year.
Minimum Amount: ₹100 per month.
Tenure: 6 months to 10 years.
Key Features: Fixed monthly installment, guaranteed maturity amount.
Best For: Salaried individuals who want to save regularly.

5. JAN DHAN ACCOUNT (PMJDY)
Purpose: Financial inclusion for unbanked Indians.
Minimum Balance: Zero (no penalty for zero balance).
Features: Free RuPay debit card, ₹10,000 overdraft, ₹2 lakh accident insurance, ₹30,000 life insurance.
Who Can Open: Any Indian citizen without a bank account.
Documents Needed: Aadhaar card only (or any identity proof).

HOW TO USE AN ATM

Step-by-Step:
1. Insert your ATM/debit card (chip-side facing the reader).
2. Enter your 4-digit PIN — cover the keypad with your other hand.
3. Select "Withdrawal" and choose "Savings Account."
4. Enter the amount (must be multiple of ₹100).
5. Collect your money and card. Always take your receipt.

Free ATM Transactions Per Month:
- SBI customers: 5 free transactions at SBI ATMs + 3 at other bank ATMs.
- Other banks: 5 free transactions at own bank ATMs + 3 at other ATMs.
- After free limit: ₹21 per transaction charge.
- Daily withdrawal limit: Usually ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 per day.

ATM Safety Rules:
- NEVER share your PIN with anyone — not even bank employees or police.
- Check for skimming devices: Pull on the card reader before inserting.
- If someone offers to "help" you at ATM — refuse and leave immediately.
- If your card gets stuck: Call your bank helpline immediately to block it. Do NOT leave the ATM.
- Card block SMS format (SBI example): BLOCK XXXX (last 4 digits) to 567676.

DIGITAL PAYMENTS — UPI

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) allows instant money transfer using just a mobile number or QR code.

How to Set Up UPI:
1. Download BHIM, PhonePe, Google Pay, or Paytm.
2. Register with your bank-linked mobile number.
3. Select your bank and verify with OTP.
4. Create a UPI PIN (4 or 6 digits) — different from ATM PIN.
5. You're ready! Share your UPI ID (like name@upi) to receive money.

UPI Limits: ₹1 lakh per transaction (₹2 lakh for verified users).

UPI SAFETY RULES:
- You NEVER need to enter your PIN to RECEIVE money. If someone asks for your PIN to send you money, it's a scam.
- "Request Money" means someone wants to TAKE money from you. Never accept unknown requests.
- Always verify the recipient name shown before confirming payment.
- Report fraud: 1800-111-109 (NPCI helpline) or cybercrime.gov.in.

NEFT vs RTGS vs IMPS vs UPI (Comparison):
- NEFT: Any amount, batch processing, 2-4 hours, free online, for regular transfers.
- RTGS: Minimum ₹2 lakh, real-time, for urgent large payments.
- IMPS: Any amount, instant, 24/7, small fee, for urgent transfers without UPI.
- UPI: Up to ₹1 lakh, instant, free, easiest to use, recommended for daily use.

KYC — KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER

KYC is mandatory for all banking services. It verifies your identity.

Documents Accepted as Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License.
Documents Accepted as Address Proof: Aadhaar Card, Utility Bill (< 3 months old), Ration Card, Bank Statement.
Photo: Recent passport-size photograph.

Video KYC: Now available — complete KYC via video call without visiting branch.

BANKING HOURS:
Monday to Friday: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Saturday: 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM (2nd and 4th Saturday are public holidays for banks).
Sunday and Public Holidays: Banks closed. ATMs and digital banking available 24/7.

- Cyber Crime/Fraud Reporting: 1930
