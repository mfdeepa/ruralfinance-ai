DIGITAL BANKING AND PAYMENTS — RuralFinance AI Knowledge Base

UPI (Unified Payments Interface): India's most popular instant money transfer system. Send money 24/7 using just a mobile number or UPI ID. Free. Instant. Works even at midnight and on holidays. Maximum Rs 1 lakh per transaction for most banks.

VPA / UPI ID (Virtual Payment Address): Your unique address on UPI. Like an email for money. Format: yourname@bankname. Example: deepa@sbi or 9876543210@paytm. Share this to receive money.

HOW UPI WORKS: Download BHIM, PhonePe, Google Pay, or Paytm. Link your bank account. Create UPI PIN (4-6 digits). Send money using receiver's UPI ID, mobile number, or QR code. Money moves instantly from your bank to their bank.

UPI PIN: A 4 or 6 digit secret number to authorize UPI transactions. Set it when linking bank account. Never share with anyone. Bank never asks for it.

BHIM APP: Government's official UPI app by NPCI. Simple, works on basic smartphones. Supports all Indian banks. Available in 20 languages.

QR CODE: A black-and-white square pattern that contains payment information. Shopkeeper shows QR code, you scan it with your phone camera to pay. No need to type account number.

NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer): Transfer money from one bank account to another. Available 24/7. Processed in batches. Takes a few minutes to a few hours. No minimum or maximum limit (bank may set limits). Free for most transactions.

RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement): Instant transfer of large amounts. Minimum Rs 2 lakh. Processed immediately in real time. Suitable for big business payments. Available 24/7.

IMPS (Immediate Payment Service): Instant money transfer 24/7 including holidays. Up to Rs 5 lakh per transaction. Slightly higher charge than NEFT. Good for urgent transfers.

COMPARISON: UPI is best for everyday payments. NEFT for regular transfers. RTGS for large amounts. IMPS for urgent medium amounts.

NET BANKING (Internet Banking): Access your bank account from a computer through the bank's website. Transfer money, pay bills, check statements, apply for loans — all from home. Secure login with username, password, and OTP.

MOBILE BANKING: Access bank through the bank's official app on your phone. Safer than net banking from public computers. Always download the official app from Play Store or App Store.

TRANSACTION ID / REFERENCE NUMBER: A unique number generated for every digital payment. Like a receipt number. Keep it safe. Used to track payments and raise complaints if money is lost.

BENEFICIARY: The person or account that receives money in a transfer. You add a beneficiary with their account number, IFSC code, and name before transferring (for NEFT/RTGS). Some banks have a 30-minute waiting period after adding beneficiary.

2-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (2FA): Two-step verification to access your account. Usually password + OTP. Much more secure than just password.

TOKENIZATION: Your card number is replaced with a random "token" for online transactions. Even if the token is stolen, it cannot be used elsewhere. Makes online shopping safer.

DIGITAL WALLET: An app where you store money digitally. Examples: Paytm wallet, PhonePe wallet, Amazon Pay. Load money from bank, spend at partner shops. Separate from UPI.

NACH (National Automated Clearing House): System for automatic recurring payments. Bank auto-deducts EMI, insurance premium, SIP on a set date every month. You give standing instruction (SI) once.

ECS (Electronic Clearing Service): Older system for automatic payments. Replaced by NACH. Used for salary credits, dividend payments.

STANDING INSTRUCTION: Instruction given to bank to automatically pay a fixed amount on a set date every month/quarter/year. Example: auto-pay electricity bill, insurance premium.

AUTO-DEBIT: Automatic deduction from your account on a specific date. Used for loan EMIs, subscriptions, utility bills. You authorize this with a mandate.

E-MANDATE: Electronic permission you give to allow auto-debit from your account. Done online with OTP verification. Replaces the old physical cheque mandate.

PAYMENT GATEWAY: The technology that processes online payments. When you pay on Amazon or Flipkart, the payment gateway (Razorpay, Paytm, CCAvenue) securely processes your card or UPI payment.

RUPAY CARD: India's own card payment network (like Visa and Mastercard). Made by NPCI. Accepted at all Indian ATMs, POS machines, and most online stores. Comes free with Jan Dhan accounts.

VISA/MASTERCARD: International payment networks. Cards with these logos work in India and abroad. Typically comes with premium bank accounts.

POS MACHINE (Point of Sale): The card-swiping machine at shops. You tap or swipe your debit/credit card to pay. PIN required for debit cards. Accepts Visa, Mastercard, RuPay.

CONTACTLESS PAYMENT (Tap and Pay): Pay by just tapping your card on the POS machine. No PIN needed for transactions up to Rs 5,000. Faster than swiping. Card has a wifi symbol printed on it.

VIRTUAL CARD: A digital card (only card number, expiry, CVV — no physical card) for online shopping. Safe because even if stolen online, your physical card is untouched.

BANK MOBILE NUMBER: The phone number linked to your bank account. OTPs come here. Always keep this updated with the bank.

SMS ALERTS: Bank sends SMS for every transaction. Always enable this. First sign of fraud if you see transactions you didn't make.

BANK PASSBOOK UPDATE via ATM: Many ATMs allow you to update your passbook. Insert passbook in the passbook printer slot at the ATM.

MISSED CALL BANKING: Dial a specific number to get balance on SMS. Example: SBI balance check — give missed call to 09223766666. Free service.

USSD BANKING (*99#): Dial *99# on any mobile (no internet needed). Works on basic keypad phones. Check balance, send money, get mini statement. Works across all Indian banks. Free.

INTERNET BANKING FRAUD PREVENTION: Never share OTP with anyone. Always type bank URL yourself — don't click links in SMS/email. Use your own device. Log out after every session. Check for https and padlock symbol in browser.
